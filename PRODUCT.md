# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Coaches, therapists, and entrepreneurs who run their business on Go High Level (or want to). SaaS platforms built on top of GHL. They are solo operators or small teams, overwhelmed by marketing execution, burned by agencies that disappeared after setup. They find kento lab through referrals or social and arrive with skepticism: "another agency?" They make decisions emotionally and validate rationally.

## Product Purpose

kento lab is a full-service marketing team for the coaching economy. Six specialists — GHL, paid media, content, and community — embedded in the client's business. The site exists to convert the skeptical into the convinced: someone lands, sees real people, real specializations, and a real offer that makes their current situation feel expensive by comparison.

## Brand Personality

Grounded. Competent. Human.

Not corporate, not hype. The voice is: a trusted colleague who also happens to be very good at their craft. Warm enough to feel personal, technical enough to feel credible. Never flashy for its own sake.

## Anti-references

- Generic agency sites with stock people and purple gradients
- SaaS-flavored B2B marketing ("scale your pipeline with AI-powered automation")
- Overly minimal "luxury" vibes that feel cold and inaccessible
- GHL reseller sites that look like GHL itself built them

## Design Principles

1. **Practice what you preach.** The site itself is proof that kento lab knows design. If we can't make our own site remarkable, why would someone trust us with theirs?
2. **Show the people.** The team is the differentiator. Real faces, real roles, real portfolios. Not headshots on cards — portraits that feel alive.
3. **Make the problem hurt first.** Visitors arrive knowing something is wrong but not knowing the solution. Make the problem visceral before offering anything.
4. **Earn the scroll.** Every section should have one visual idea that couldn't exist on a Squarespace template. Commit to the craft.
5. **Warmth through specificity.** Generic copy is cold. Named technologies, named outcomes, named people — that's where warmth lives.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Reduced-motion alternative for all animations (already implemented via prefers-reduced-motion). Spanish-speaking team serving English-speaking clients — clarity and plain language matter.
